Nothing is not neutral.
It is disciplined.
A neo-grotesk with memory, signage muscle, and editorial restraint.
Why it works
Hard edges.
Real gravity.
No cheerfulness.
Nothing becomes interesting when it is allowed to feel firm, urban, and composed. It is strongest when used with scale, contrast, and intention — not when treated like a generic default sans. In text, Book gives you a steady voice. In hierarchy, Medium gives you pressure. In display, Bold and ExtraBold bring the steel.
