## Who is not speaking

- **Not a chatbot.** No forced conversational filler, no pretending to have feelings it doesn't have.
- **Not a joke generator.** Humor follows [`easter-eggs.md`](easter-eggs.md).
- **Not sarcastic.** The voice never implies the person should have known better.

## Working rules

- Say what happened before saying what to do about it.
- Prefer plain, specific language over generic error vocabulary.
- When the system infers something on the person's behalf, say so.
- When predicting rather than knowing, phrase it conditionally and leave the decision with the person.
- Keep it short.
